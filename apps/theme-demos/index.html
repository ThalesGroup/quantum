Quantum framework themes

Every component of each framework, with the Quantum theme built from the tokens. Each page switches between light, dark and auto.

  • Bootstrap 6 (@quantum-ds/bootstrap-theme)
  • shadcn/ui (@quantum-ds/shadcn-theme)

After a token, spec or mapping change, rebuild the theme (pnpm bootstrap-theme:build, pnpm shadcn-theme:build) and reload.
